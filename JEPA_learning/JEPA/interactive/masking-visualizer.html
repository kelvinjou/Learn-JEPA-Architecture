What to notice The paper reports an average context ratio of 0.25 at a 14×14 grid. Per image this sampler gives 0.41 — read the histogram. The gap is the batch-minimum truncation. Set G = 14 and slide the batch to 128, the paper's per-GPU batch size, and the "effective ratio" line lands at about 0.26. So the paper's 0.25 is not a property of the mask sampler; it is a property of the sampler plus the batching, and it therefore depends on a hyperparameter nobody reports alongside it.
Trap Push the grid down to 8 and the batch up to 256, as you would for CIFAR-10. The effective ratio falls to about 0.17 — well under the paper's 0.25 — because with only 64 patches one unlucky image drags the whole batch down. On a coarse grid you have to raise context_scale or lower the batch size, and you should know which one you changed.
Try to break it Find a setting where the context is empty for some images. Then look at what min_keep does about it: the real sampler retries up to 20 times per constraint level, then starts dropping constraints — it will let the context overlap a target rather than return nothing. Set min_keep high and watch how often that has to happen.
